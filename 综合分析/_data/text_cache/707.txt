   Abstract—Fifth Generation (5G) and beyond networks rely on
the HTTP/2 protocol for signaling between core Network Func-
tions (NFs). While HTTP/2 vulnerabilities have been exploited to
perform various types of Denial of Service (DoS) attacks in web en-
vironments, their impact on telecommunication networks remains
under-studied. Though secure by design, the 5G Service-Based
Architecture (SBA) can be vulnerable to misconfigurations and vir-
tualization exploits, particularly with Mobile Network Operators
(MNOs) using hyper-scale technologies. This work addresses the
