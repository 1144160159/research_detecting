Based on these considerations, we believe that Deep Learning
(DL), which allows to train classifiers directly from input
data by automatically learning structured (and complex) fea-
ture representations [17], may be the stepping stone toward
high-performing TC in the dynamic and challenging mobile
context.
   However, a naïve adoption of DL techniques to (mobile) TC
may imply misleading design choices and lead to biased con-
clusions, due to the peculiar (and tricky) nature of network
traffic data. This constitutes, in our opinion, one of the
main gaps to fill (viz. the prerequisite) for the capitaliza-
tion of DL assets to mobile TC, thus echoing its successful
use in “mature” fields, e.g., image and natural language
processing [17].
