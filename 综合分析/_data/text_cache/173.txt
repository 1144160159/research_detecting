                Nan Wei, Lihua Yin , Jingyi Tan, Chuhong Ruan, Chuang Yin, Zhe Sun , and Xi Luo



   Abstract—Cyber-attacks have become more frequent, targeted,
and complex as the exponential growth in computer networks and
the development of Internet of Things (IoT). Network intrusion
detection system (NIDS) is an important and essential tool to
protect network environments. However, the low performance of
a NIDS against small malicious samples has seriously threatened
the security of networks, thus directly leading to the loss of
personal property and national interests. Given this, we propose
an auto encoder-based hybrid detection model, abbreviated as
AHDM, for the intrusion detection with small-sample problem.
