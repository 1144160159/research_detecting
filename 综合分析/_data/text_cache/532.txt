   Abstract—To identify pattern deviations in large-scale industrial
infrastructures, anomaly detection is crucial yet challenging. Pre-
vious research has not adequately addressed the characteristics
and deployment considerations in these complex scenarios. In this
paper, we present InoU, a scalable anomaly detection framework
to process unlabeled multivariate time-series data. We incorporate
a VAE filter to ease impacts from noisy components in training ma-
terials. We propose a scalable trio-attention U-Transformer to con-
struct the typical representation of high-dimensional streams and
