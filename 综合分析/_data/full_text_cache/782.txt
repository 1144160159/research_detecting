disturbances. Simultaneously, it aims to minimize the H∞
norm of the difference between the designed residual generator
and this reference model. With model parameters estimated via
Bayesian inference, modelling errors are treated as stochastic
uncertainties, leading to an LMI solution originally derived in
Chapter 8 in [21].
